A letter for my favorite person

Dear Christy,

Baby, even though we sometimes fight and have misunderstandings, I hope u never think that I will leave u because of ur attitude or because of the things that make us argue. I want u to know that no matter how difficult things become between us, I will always choose to love u and stay by ur side. I will love u until the very end, and I promise that I will never give up on us just because things get hard. That is my promise to u, to our relationship, and to the Lord, who knows how sincere my love for u is.

I have promised myself that this relationship is something I want to keep for a lifetime, baby. I don't want us to be just another love story that ends when problems come. I want us to grow together, learn from our mistakes, and become better people for each other.

I'm really sorry if there are times when I keep making u angry, when I'm too playful, too stubborn, or sometimes too difficult to understand. I'm sorry for the moments when my actions or words make u feel upset, even when hurting u is never my intention. I know that I still have many things to improve about myself, and I want u to know that I'm willing to learn because u and our relationship mean so much to me.

Baby, let's always remember the words we keep telling each other whenever things aren't okay between us "we fight, we talk, we fix" Let's never forget that, because arguments should never be the reason we stop choosing each other. Instead of letting our misunderstandings destroy what we have, let's use them as lessons to understand each other better. Let's always communicate, listen to each other's feelings, and remember that we're on the same team, even when we disagree.

I know we won't always have perfect days, and there will be times when we struggle to understand each other, but I hope we never forget the love that brought us together. I want to celebrate more monthsaries with u, make beautiful memories with u, and reach all the dreams we've been talking about together. I pray to the Lord that He continues to guide our relationship and gives us the patience, strength, and understanding we need to overcome every challenge.

Christy, u are so precious to me, and I hope my actions will always show u how much I love u. No matter how many times we disagree, my heart will always choose to work things out with u. I love u so, so much, Christy, more than words can ever explain!

With all my love, always yours 💜
